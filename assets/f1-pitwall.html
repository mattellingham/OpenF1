Navigation
TabsSwitch between Overview, Live, Replay, Championship and Schedule using the navigation bar.
OverviewAt-a-glance view of the next race countdown, last race podium, and top-5 standings.
ChampionshipFull driver and constructor standings tables for the current season.
ScheduleFull season calendar. Click any completed race to view its full results.
Live Session
Auto-pollData updates automatically — locations every 2.5s, car telemetry every 5s, positions every 10s, race control every 20s.
Track MapCar positions on the circuit. Drivers in the pits or retired appear as badges below the map.
Driver FocusClick driver pills at the top to highlight up to 3 drivers. Other drivers dim in tables and on the map.
Driver DetailClick any row in the timing table to open a detailed modal with lap charts, tyre strategy, pit history, and a driver comparison tool.
BannerSignificant events (flags, safety car, DRS) appear in a scrollable banner above the cards.
Card VisibilityUse the "Cards ▾" dropdown to show/hide individual cards, show all, hide all, or reset to session-type defaults.
NotificationsClick the 🔔 bell icon to enable browser notifications for red flags, safety cars, and session events when the tab is in the background.
Session-Adaptive Cards
PracticeTiming, Best Laps, Sector Times, Run Analysis, Lap Chart, Speed Trap, Teammate H2H, Telemetry, Team Radio.
QualifyingTiming, Quali Classification, Quali Tracker, Sector Times, Tyre Stints, Speed Trap, Teammate H2H, Telemetry, Team Radio.
Race / SprintTiming, Pit Stops, Tyre Stints, Lap Chart, Speed Trap, Pit Window, Race Pace, Battles, Strategy Simulator, Teammate H2H, Telemetry, Team Radio.
Replay Mode
Session BrowserPick a year and session. Data is downloaded and cached in your browser for instant future loads.
PlaybackUse the timeline bar at the bottom to play, pause, and scrub through the session.
SpeedAdjust playback speed from 1× to 60× using the speed buttons in the replay bar.
Track MapCar positions are interpolated at 60fps for smooth movement during replay playback.
ExportSave a downloaded session as a JSON file to load later without re-downloading.
Compare SessionsClick "⚖ Compare Sessions" in the session browser to load two cached sessions side-by-side. Compare lap times, sector times, and pace across different years or events.
Customising Layout
ColumnsUse the column buttons (2–6) at the top of Live or Replay to change the grid layout.
Card SpanEach card has a column-span dropdown in its header — set how many columns a card occupies.
Drag & DropGrab the ⠿ handle on any card header to reorder cards by dragging.
CollapseClick the ▾ chevron to collapse a card. Your layout, order, spans, and collapse state are all saved.
Resize HeightDrag the bottom edge of any card body to resize its height. Heights are saved per card.
Keyboard Shortcuts
?Toggle this help panel.
EscClose any open modal or help panel.
1–5Switch to Overview (1), Live (2), Replay (3), Championship (4), Schedule (5).
TToggle between dark and light theme.
PPause/resume live data polling.
